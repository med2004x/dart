# Lesson NN: Title

## Objective

State exactly what the learner should understand or be able to do.

## Prerequisites

List the previous lesson or external prerequisite.

## Learn

Use a short explanation, derivation, diagram, or reference. Do not duplicate a full textbook.

## Attempt

Give a concrete task. The starting files should not contain the solution.

## Verify

Require tests, traces, calculations, benchmarks, invariants, or another observable form of evidence.

## Failure drill

Break the system or assumption deliberately and diagnose it.

## Transfer

Solve the same idea in a different domain or under one changed constraint.

## Done when

State the understanding test in observable terms.
